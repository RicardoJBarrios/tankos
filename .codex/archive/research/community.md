# Community Evidence

## Repeated problems

Across Reddit and Reef2Reef, users repeatedly mention:

- paper, notes and spreadsheets remain easier than some apps for simple logging;
- apps feel clunky, bloated, dated or hard to navigate;
- users stop measuring frequently once a tank stabilizes;
- multiple tanks make comparison and organization harder;
- subscription limits and cloud reliability affect trust;
- manual entry is still common because integrations are incomplete;
- users want trends connected to actions, not isolated numbers.

Examples: [multi-tank tracking discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Aquariums/comments/1rivvt0/how_do_you_track_water_parameters_for_multiple/),
[app dissatisfaction discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Aquariums/comments/1rpe7mv/what_app_would_you_recommend/),
[Reef2Reef app comparison](https://www.reef2reef.com/threads/best-aquarium-maintenance-log-apps-for-android.839751/).

Nano-Reef was included in the search scope, but no current, sufficiently
specific indexed thread was found to support a product conclusion. It remains a
source gap rather than evidence of absence.

## Requested or emerging capabilities

The sample mentions quick entry, simple graphs, import from spreadsheets or other
apps, better multi-tank comparison, parameter insights, photos, maintenance
reminders and integrations. It also shows interest in AI assistants, but AI
quality and safety are not established by enthusiasm alone.

## Abandoned or avoided behavior

Many users stop using detailed logging after a tank stabilizes, rely on visual
observation, or keep a spreadsheet only during cycling/troubleshooting. This is
not evidence that history has no value; it is evidence that continuous manual
logging has weak retention.

## Critical interpretation

Community posts are not a representative survey. They support hypotheses about
friction and workflow, not market-size or feature-priority claims. The strongest
next research is interviews with new, stable, multi-tank and controller-led
keepers.
