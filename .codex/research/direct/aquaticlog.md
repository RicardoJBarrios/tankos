# AquaticLog

**Positioning:** long-running multi-aquarium web/mobile tracker for freshwater,
planted and reef users.

## Observed product

The current store listing emphasizes multiple aquariums, readings, photos,
reminders, tank switching and configurable widgets. The product is subscription
based: the listing shows monthly, yearly and lifetime Pro options.
[App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquaticlog/id583895470?platform=ipad)

Community references describe parameter graphs, diary, maintenance and dosing,
custom parameter ranges and sharing/private aquarium data.
[Reef2Reef discussion](https://www.reef2reef.com/threads/whats-your-favorite-app-to-track-your-tank.956948/)

## Model and UX pattern

The pattern is Aquarium → readings, diary, maintenance, livestock/media and
sharing. The core value is a long-lived log and cross-device access rather than
controller automation.

## Strengths

- Longevity and multi-tank orientation.
- Graphs, diary and sharing support historical review.
- Broad freshwater-to-reef positioning.

## Weaknesses and signals

Store reviews mention the free limit, pricing, reminder/range accuracy and
difficulty correcting entries. Users also report abandoning apps for paper or
spreadsheets after a tank stabilizes.
[App Store review excerpt](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquaticlog/id583895470?platform=ipad)

## Strategic implication

TankOS should not assume frequent measurement is the universal job. It should
support episodic logging and make the reason for a record clear: baseline,
change, problem investigation or routine care.
