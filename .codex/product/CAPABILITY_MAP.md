# Candidate Capability Map

This is a product map, not a bounded-context map or delivery commitment.

| Functional area | Candidate capabilities | Why it belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care context | Establish and identify an Aquarium context. | Gives care information a meaningful subject. |
| Care records | Record Measurements, Observations and Inspections. | Captures information needed to understand care. |
| Care planning | Plan Tasks, record Maintenance and surface Reminders. | Supports recurring and completed care work. |
| Living and equipment | Record Livestock and Equipment associations. | Connects care information to the things being cared for. |
| History and understanding | Consult a Timeline and relevant past records. | Helps a keeper interpret current status and past action. |
| Trust and control | Authentication, authorization, ownership, collaboration, export and deletion. | Protects private care information and user control. |
| Assisted care | Alerts, sensor input, automation and AI assistance. | May add value later, but needs safety and evidence first. |

No area is a definitive bounded context. Split or combine areas only when
validated use cases reveal separate language, ownership or consistency needs.
