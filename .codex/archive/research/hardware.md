# Hardware and Automation Ecosystem

## Neptune Apex / Fusion

Apex combines critical parameter monitoring, device control, power monitoring,
alerts and programmable decisions through Apex Fusion. The product's value is
life-support automation and remote safety, not a general husbandry journal.
[A3 series](https://www.neptunesystems.com/apex-a3-series/)

## GHL ProfiLux / GHL Connect / myGHL

GHL offers local web access, cloud access, app and desktop software. ProfiLux
supports lighting, pH, conductivity/salinity, ORP, temperature, level, flow,
leakage, dosing and alarms. GHL explicitly emphasizes local operation without a
cloud dependency. [ProfiLux 4](https://www.aquariumcomputer.com/products/profilux-aquarium-controller/profilux-4/)

## Reef Factory Smart Reef

Smart Reef centralizes Reef Factory devices, dashboards, parameter tables,
measurement history, planned jobs, device history and alerts. It is a vertically
integrated product ecosystem. [Smart Reef](https://reeffactory.com/en/smart-reef-app/)

## HYDROS

HYDROS supports multiple aquariums/devices, tile/text/graph views, outlets,
schedules, modes, archive/clone and controls for ATO, pumps, lights, heaters and
other equipment. It emphasizes no-code presets and device-centered control.
[App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hydros-app/id1455344294)

## Home Assistant and ESPHome

Home Assistant provides a general local automation layer; ESPHome provides
device firmware and sensor preprocessing, including units, accuracy and filters.
[ESPHome sensors](https://esphome.io/components/sensor/)

Aquarium projects such as AquaPi and Marine Assistant show that hobbyists build
local sensor/control systems with temperature, level, leak, pH, salinity and
relay control. [AquaPi](https://therealfalsereality.github.io/aquapi/)

## Ecosystem pattern

The hardware market owns live state and actuation. A neutral keeper product would
need to ingest events from several vendors, preserve source/provenance and avoid
becoming a safety controller. Direct control should remain out of scope until a
specific integration and safety model exist.
