# Research Methodology

## Scope

The sample covers aquarium logging apps, controller ecosystems, open-source
projects, adjacent care/maintenance products and hobbyist communities.

## Evidence levels

- **Product fact:** stated by the product owner or store listing.
- **Observed criticism:** a user review or forum comment; useful signal, not a
  population estimate.
- **Pattern:** repeated across independent products or discussions.
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** an inference that still needs interviews or tests.

## Limits

Feature pages are marketing claims and may not describe reliability. Store
ratings are platform-, version- and survivorship-biased. Forum threads are
self-selected and often old. Open-source project activity does not prove user
adoption. No market size, revenue or retention data was available in public
sources.

## Source policy

Prefer official product pages, app-store listings and project repositories for
capabilities and pricing. Use Reddit, Reef2Reef and other forums only for
workflows, frustrations and requests. Prices and availability are snapshots and
must be rechecked before commercial decisions.
