# Critical Scope Recommendation

This recommendation is based on the research sample, not validated customer
discovery. It should be revisited after interviews and a usability prototype.

## MVP candidate

1. Establish an Aquarium. An initial product release may cap the number of
   Aquariums at one; that is not a domain-model constraint.
2. Record a small, user-selected set of manual observations or measurements with
   explicit time, unit and source context.
3. Record a small set of care actions or notes.
4. Review a focused history that connects records by time and subject.
5. Work for episodic use: cycling, a change, a concern or routine care, without
   requiring continuous daily measurement.

**Why:** these capabilities address the repeated paper/spreadsheet problem,
quick-entry friction, historical troubleshooting and data trust. They are also
small enough to validate before livestock libraries, automation or AI.

## Later, after MVP evidence

- Care tasks and flexible reminders.
- Livestock and Equipment associations.
- Better trend views and activity overlays.
- Multiple Aquarium contexts.
- Sharing if interviews confirm the need. Authentication is required earlier by
  the accepted product decision because the first durable Aquarium is private;
  this supersedes the research sequencing hypothesis.
- Export and restore after the product accepts portability scope, authorization
  and recovery semantics.

These features are common and useful, but they should follow validation of the
core logging/history loop.

## Defer

- Device integrations and controller imports.
- Collaboration and complex permissions.
- Alerts, health scores and recommendations.
- Image processing, species identification and AI.
- Dosing calculators, marketplace, social features and advanced analytics.

They require stronger evidence, external data quality, safety, privacy or
operational commitments.

## Do not implement as part of Veril's core

- A universal life-support controller.
- A generic social network or marketplace.
- A universal “safe” health score or autonomous treatment advice.
- A large reference database without a sustainable editorial model.

These are either served by existing ecosystems or create liability and scope
without evidence of a Veril-specific advantage.

## Critical caveat

“MVP” here is a research hypothesis, not a product decision. The next validation
should observe new, stable, multi-tank and controller-led keepers completing a
real record-and-review task and compare Veril with their current notebook,
spreadsheet or app.
