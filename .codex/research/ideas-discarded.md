# Ideas Not Supported as Differentiators

These ideas are not necessarily bad products. They are rejected as Veril
differentiators for now because the evidence shows they are already common,
high-risk or unsupported by a specific unmet need.

| Idea | Why not now |
| --- | --- |
| Another generic all-in-one aquarium tracker | Aquarimate, AquaticLog, Pocket Marine and newer apps already cover the feature bundle. |
| Large species encyclopedia | Established products already have extensive reference data; maintaining accuracy is a separate business. |
| Universal safe-range health score | Users and products disagree on ranges; false certainty could cause harm. |
| Generic AI aquarium chat | New products already offer it, but accuracy and privacy evidence are weak. |
| Direct control of all hardware | Apex, GHL, HYDROS and Reef Factory already own control; safety and vendor APIs create high risk. |
| Social feed or marketplace | Present in parts of the market but not tied to the core record-keeping problem. |
| Continuous measurement as the primary habit | Community evidence shows many keepers stop testing after stabilization. |
| Complex CQRS/event-sourcing product architecture | No market evidence requires it; it would solve a technical problem before a product one. |
