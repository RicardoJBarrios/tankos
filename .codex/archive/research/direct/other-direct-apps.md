# Other Direct Products

## Aquarium Log

An aesthetically focused aquarium journal advertises parameter recording,
calendar notes, alerts, cloud backup and unlimited tanks.
[Product page](https://www.aquariumlog.app/about)

## Saltwater Aquarium Logger

This web product combines parameters, ICP uploads, equipment/receipt storage,
livestock, dosing, water changes, public sharing and a marketplace.
[Product page](https://sal.fish/)

## Reef Theory and ReefLog pattern

Newer products repeatedly market “clean”, “focused”, “local-first” or “quick
logging”, suggesting that the established feature set is not itself sufficient
to make data entry feel worthwhile.

## Open-source freshwater projects

Search results show small projects built around water tests, fish/plant inventory
and simple free/offline operation. Their existence supports demand for low-cost
logging, but public repositories and posts do not provide reliable adoption
metrics.
[Example discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Aquariums/comments/1l70o9p/built_an_aquarium_water_test_tracking_app_streamlit_python/)
