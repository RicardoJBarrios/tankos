# Product Privacy Strategy

This is a product-level baseline, not legal advice or a completed compliance
program. Concrete obligations depend on jurisdictions, product scope and
accepted data uses.

## Data categories

- **Personal and account data:** identity and contact data needed for access;
  exact fields are pending.
- **Care data:** Aquarium, Livestock, Equipment, Measurements, Observations and
  care-work information may be sensitive to the keeper.
- **Images:** may include subjects, embedded metadata and inferred location or
  health information.
- **Analytics and diagnostics:** must be minimized and kept separate from domain
  history.
- **AI inputs and outputs:** require explicit purpose, consent, provenance,
  retention and human control before use.

## Principles

- Collect and retain only information needed by an accepted use case.
- Make ownership, access, export and deletion behavior understandable before
  collecting private information.
- Do not enable analytics, AI processing or image analysis by default merely
  because a provider supports it.
- Do not treat local persistence as a secure private store on a shared device.
- Review privacy, retention and user control whenever a new data category or
  integration is proposed.

## Pending decisions

Retention periods, deletion guarantees, export scope, legal basis, consent
records, privacy notices and incident response require product and legal review.
