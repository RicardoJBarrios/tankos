# Indirect Competition and Transferable Patterns

These products solve adjacent problems. They are useful for interaction and
retention patterns, not evidence that Veril should copy their domains.

## Gardening and plant care

Plant Diary combines a plant collection, care actions, custom schedules, notes,
photos and tasks. [Features](https://plantdiaryapp.com/features)

Transferable pattern: the cared-for subject owns its history and routine.
Warning: aquarium parameters have source, unit and safety semantics that plant
watering reminders do not.

## IoT and smart-home control

Home Assistant plus ESPHome emphasizes local devices, sensor preprocessing,
calibration, unit metadata, filters and automation. AquaPi demonstrates the
aquarium-specific version with water-level and water-quality sensors.
[ESPHome sensor model](https://esphome.io/components/sensor/),
[AquaPi discussion](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/aquapi-for-esphome/782939)

Transferable pattern: source, unit, accuracy and connectivity status belong next
to telemetry. Warning: a home-automation dashboard is not a care history or a
safe domain model.

## Maintenance and asset management

Asset-management products organize assets, locations, recurring schedules,
history, work and offline field activity. [Zapium](https://www.zapium.com/)

Transferable pattern: planned work, actual work, asset history and recurrence
should not be conflated. Warning: business-asset roles and work orders do not
justify aquarium roles or workflows.

## Laboratory and ELN products

Electronic lab notebooks combine rich entries, templates, sample tracking,
collaboration and audit trails. [ELabELN](https://www.elabeln.com/elabeln/)

Transferable pattern: provenance, actor, time, correction history and export
matter when records must be trusted. Warning: aquarium hobby data does not yet
justify regulated audit, signatures or laboratory complexity.

## Pet and animal-care tracking

Pet-care apps combine subject profiles, health records, medication/vaccine
reminders, weight history, photos, family sharing and exportable reports.
[Keia](https://keia.app/), [Pawza](https://lagerland-apps.github.io/apps/pawza/)

Transferable pattern: a cared-for subject, recurring actions, observations and a
shareable history form a coherent care record. Warning: aquarium measurements
and water chemistry need explicit units/provenance rather than a generic health
diary.

## Indirect conclusion

The strongest transferable idea is a subject-centered, provenance-aware care
record with optional routines and export. The strongest non-transferable idea is
the assumption that every care domain benefits from reminders, scores or AI.
