# User Journeys

These journeys describe outcomes, not screens, technical flows or commitments.

## Accepted: Establish an Aquarium

An authenticated keeper establishes one private Aquarium with a name, receives a
clear confirmation and can later return to it as the context for care. Public
presentation, sharing, Display/System setup and recovery behavior are deferred.

## Record a Parameter

A keeper notices or measures a Parameter, records the value in the relevant
Aquarium context, and can later understand what was recorded and when. Units,
source, correction and offline behavior remain pending.

## Plan care work

A keeper identifies work that should be done, records an intention, and later
records or reviews its outcome. Recurrence, completion, cancellation and
reminders are hypotheses until specified.

## Add Livestock

A keeper records that Livestock is associated with an Aquarium so future care
has context. Individual versus group, transfer and lifecycle behavior are open.

## Consult history

A keeper reviews relevant past records to understand the current situation or
decide what to do next. The scope, ordering and retention of the Timeline must
follow accepted use cases rather than an assumed universal history.
