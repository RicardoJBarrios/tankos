# Reef Log and Reef Theory

These newer products show that the category is still active and converging on a
focused, fast logging experience.

## ReefLog

The Google Play listing positions ReefLog as local-first, saltwater-focused and
fast to log. It advertises focused parameters, recent readings, target ranges,
health status, trends, dosing history, maintenance reminders, local backup and
export. [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nedelyalabs.reeflog)

The implication is important: local backup/export is marketed as a core trust
feature rather than an afterthought. This is product evidence, not proof of
retention or reliability.

## Reef Theory

Reef Theory is in private beta and explicitly replaces scattered notes,
spreadsheets and reminders. It combines parameters, livestock, care tasks,
dosing, water changes and trends across reef, freshwater and planted tanks; it
lists automation integrations, advanced analytics and community as future work.
[Product page](https://reeftheoryaquatics.com/)

## Strategic implication

The market is moving toward “focused and quick” while still adding breadth.
TankOS needs a sharper promise than a generic all-in-one tracker and should treat
local-first/export and contextual history as hypotheses worth testing.
