# Rule Catalogue

No product rule is accepted unless an approved specification states it. This
catalogue keeps categories separate while discovery proceeds.

## Validation rules

Candidate rules about completeness, format, range, Unit and internal consistency
of information supplied for a use case.

## Business rules

Candidate rules about what care work means, which associations are valid, and
which facts may change or require history.

## Security rules

Candidate rules about authentication, authorization, ownership and collaboration
for each action. No role names or permission matrix are accepted.

## Synchronization rules

Candidate rules about conflict behavior, ordering, idempotency and current-value
checks for a specific accepted operation.

## Offline rules

Candidate rules that classify each accepted operation as available offline,
read-only offline or online-required.

## Interface rules

Product conventions that information must be understandable, accessible and
clear about absent, stale or pending state. Detailed interaction behavior remains
use-case-specific.
