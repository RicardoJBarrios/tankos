# Pocket Marine

**Positioning:** paid marine reference and personal logbook focused on practical
reef keeping.

## Observed product

It combines an 800+ species encyclopedia, 30+ parameter journal, trends,
inhabitant diary, dosing, reminders, water-change and maintenance journals,
calculators and compatibility guidance. [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocket-marine/id999538938)

## Model and UX pattern

The apparent model is Tank → tests, inhabitants, dosing, maintenance and
reminders, with a species knowledge layer. A user review praises the UI but
criticizes fixed dashboard parameters and hard-to-read graph navigation.

## Strengths

- Focused marine vocabulary and reference content.
- High rating and simple single-purpose positioning.
- Local/no-data-collected posture stated by the store listing.

## Weaknesses and signals

The public evidence points to limited customization of dashboard parameters and
chart navigation friction. It is primarily an iOS/iPad product, which limits
cross-platform reach.

## Strategic implication

Contextual defaults are valuable, but must be editable. Veril could differentiate
by making the “why this value matters” and “what changed around it” easier to
understand without reproducing a large species encyclopedia.
